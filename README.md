# Album 122 — The Serpent Spoke
Eastward in Eden

Eight songs on the serpent, the fruit, Adam and Eve, and the guarded way. Tamil and English. Sense kept.
 
# Track List:
1. The Serpent Spoke (Narrative – Genesis 3; later identified as the devil, Rev 12:9)
2. Has God Indeed Said? (Teaching)
3. The Fruit of the Tree (Poetic – real fruit of the forbidden tree)
4. In the Day You Eat (Warning)
5. Adam Formed, Eve Built (Narrative – Genesis 2)
6. Eastward He Planted (Teaching on location and “midst of the garden”)
7. Naked and Not Ashamed (Before the fall)
8. The Way Guarded (Cherubim and flaming sword)

Study note: The serpent is a spiritual adversary acting in the narrative; the fruit is the real fruit of the tree God forbade; Adam and Eve are the first human pair placed in the garden.

Prayer note: Lord, keep us from the voice that questions Your word.
